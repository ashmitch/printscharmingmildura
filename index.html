About Us

25 years of custom framing

Prints Charming has been providing outstanding custom framing for 25 years. It all started way back in 1997 with a little shop in a small arcade.

We can frame anything! From footy jumpers to wedding bouquets, boxing gloves to cricket bats, grandad's war medals — we've even framed a brick before.

Based in Mildura, Victoria, we invite you to pay us a visit, see our showroom and browse our wide range of artwork, mirrors and custom framing options.
